Space Terra privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers the Space Terra Android app and the web game at spaceterra.herokuapp.com. Space Terra is a hobby project by Haramrit Singh Khurana.

What the game stores

  • Leaderboard entries. When a run ends, the pilot name you chose and your score are sent to the Space Terra server and stored in its database. The top scores are shown publicly to every player, so do not use your real name or any personal information as a pilot name.
  • On your device. The Android app keeps your pilot name, best score, coins, upgrades and sound setting on your phone. Apart from Android's own encrypted backup to your Google account (which restores it on a new phone), it never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Web sign-in. If you sign in with Google on the website, the server receives your Google display name, email address and profile picture to greet you and label your scores. These are kept only in your browser session. While you are signed in, your coins, best score and upgrades are saved on the Space Terra server under your Google account ID so they follow you to other browsers.
  • Web as a guest. Without signing in, your coins, best score and upgrades stay in your browser's local storage.

Services from Google

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the Android app. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions in order to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time under Privacy settings on the main menu. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play Games Services provides achievements and a Play Games leaderboard. If you use it, Google processes your Play Games profile under the Google Privacy Policy, and the app may use your Play Games name as your default pilot name.
  • Google Analytics counts page views on the website.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Space Terra is not directed at children under 13.

Removing your data

To have leaderboard entries or saved progress removed, open an issue at github.com/haramrit09k/spaceterra/issues with the pilot name and approximate scores, or use the contact email on the Google Play listing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
